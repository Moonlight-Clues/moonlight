COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION of 30.4.2026 withdrawing, at the holder's request, the conditional marketing authorisation granted by Decision C(2022) 6194 final for "Roctavian - valoctocogene roxaparvovec", an orphan medicinal product for human use
Having regard to the application submitted by BioMarin International Limited on 6 March 2026 with a view to the withdrawal of the marketing authorisation for the medicinal product "Roctavian - valoctocogene roxaparvovec",
(1) The placing on the market of the medicinal product "Roctavian - valoctocogene roxaparvovec", which is entered in the Union Register of Medicinal Products under the number EU/1/22/1668 was authorised by Commission Decision C(2022) 6194 final of 24 August 2022.
(2) Following the holder's request, that authorisation should be withdrawn,
Article 1
At the holder's request, the marketing authorisation granted by Decision C(2022) 6194 final of 24 August 2022 for the medicinal product "Roctavian - valoctocogene roxaparvovec" is withdrawn.
Article 2
The withdrawal referred to in Article 1 shall be applicable with effect from 29 May 2026.
