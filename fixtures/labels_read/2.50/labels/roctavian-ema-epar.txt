EMA, EPAR page "Roctavian" (EMA product number EMEA/H/C/005830). Status shown: "Authorised". "This medicine is authorised for use in the European Union". "This page was last updated on 14/01/2026".
Therapeutic indication
Treatment of severe haemophilia A (congenital factor VIII deficiency) in adult patients without a history of factor VIII inhibitors and without detectable antibodies to adeno-associated virus serotype 5 (AAV5).
Other information
Roctavian received a conditional marketing authorisation valid throughout the EU on 24 August 2022.
Withdrawn or no longer marketed: this page says neither. It was last updated before the European Commission's withdrawal decision of 30 April 2026 (see ../withdrawal/european-commission-decision-c-2026-3038.txt).
