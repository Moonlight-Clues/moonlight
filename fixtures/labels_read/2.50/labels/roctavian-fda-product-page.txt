FDA, Vaccines, Blood, & Biologics: ROCTAVIAN. STN: 125720. Proper Name: valoctocogene roxaparvovec-rvox. Tradename: ROCTAVIAN. Manufacturer: BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc. "Content current as of: 10/08/2025".
Indication
Indicated for the treatment of adults with severe hemophilia A (congenital factor VIII deficiency with factor VIII activity <1 IU/dL) without pre-existing antibodies to adeno-associated virus serotype 5 detected by an FDA-approved test.
Withdrawn or no longer marketed: the page says neither. No FDA record of a revoked licence was found: the Federal Register (its search API, terms "roctavian" and "valoctocogene", asked 2026-11-25) holds no such notice.
