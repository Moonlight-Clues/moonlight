Marketing category: unapproved drug other. Marketing start date: 07/10/2010. Marketing end date: 09/30/2011. Label revised 2/2019 (version 3). Not returned by DailyMed's current-label list (services/v2/spls.json?setid=...: 0 results, checked 2026-11-25).
Active ingredients: CHROMIUM PICOLINATE 1000 ug; BIOTIN 2 mg; PYRIDOXINE 25 mg; CYANOCOBALAMIN 15 ug; FOLIC ACID 1 mg; OMEGA-3-ACID ETHYL ESTERS 300 mg.
The label has no INDICATIONS AND USAGE section. Its first paragraph (the only place it says what the product is for) reads:
DivistaTM is specially designed as a once a day nutritional adjuvant to the current standard of care for patients with, or at risk of developing type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). DivistaTM provides a method for reducing hyperglycemia and stabilizing the level of serum cholesterol.
