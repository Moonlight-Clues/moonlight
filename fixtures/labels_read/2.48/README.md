# Ticket 2.48: the 23 wrong source links

Ticket 2.45 found 23 Approved cards whose "Source record" opened a label for a
different product, while another current label of the same medicine names
the disease (fixtures/labels_read/2.45/README.md, section "Wrong source link").
This ticket adds one entry per card to moonlight/confirmed.json (ticket 2.42),
dated 2026-11-25, so each card shows exactly that label as its Source record.

- The labels were NOT fetched again: every one of the 23 saved files in
  fixtures/labels_read/2.45/wrong_source/ was there and readable. Its first
  line is the new link, and the quoted sentence below is in it word for word.
- Each disease's own list was asked live on 2026-11-25 (Open Targets data
  26.09) and saved in disease_drugs/. Every card was still on its disease's
  main Approved list, in no folded box, with no grey line, and showed the old
  link. None was skipped. No card had the no-regulator grey line.
- The diseases' names and exact synonyms, asked live the same day, are in
  diseases.response.json. Two sentences use another name for the disease that
  the database itself lists as an exact synonym: "chronic inflammatory
  demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP)" (MONDO_0006702) and "juvenile
  arthritis" (MONDO_0011429).
- Migraine: acetaminophen, aspirin and caffeine all point at Excedrin
  Migraine, a combination product of the three. Kept, as 2.45 found.
- Paroxetine is here three times (OCD, panic disorder, PTSD), all to Paxil.
- Three old links were EMA pages (pasireotide, efgartigimod alfa,
  risankizumab). They are replaced by DailyMed labels too.
