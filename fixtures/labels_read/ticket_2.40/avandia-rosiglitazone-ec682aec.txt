AVANDIA 2MG- rosiglitazone maleate tablet, film coated
INDICATIONS AND USAGE
AVANDIA is a thiazolidinedione antidiabetic agent indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus. ( 1 )
Important Limitations of Use:
•
AVANDIA should not be used in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus or for the treatment of diabetic ketoacidosis. ( 1 )
•
Coadministration of AVANDIA and insulin is not recommended. ( 1 , 5.1 , 5.2 )
