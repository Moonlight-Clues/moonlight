ISEMBYLD- apitegromab-mstn injection
1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE
ISEMBYLD is indicated for the treatment of spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) in adults and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older who are currently receiving a survival motor neuron 2 (SMN2)-targeted treatment.
