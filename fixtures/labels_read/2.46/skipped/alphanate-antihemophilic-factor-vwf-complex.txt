Alphanate- antihemophilic factor/von willebrand factor complex (human) kit
1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE
ALPHANATE is indicated for:
Treatment and prevention of bleeding episodes and perioperative management in adult and pediatric patients with Factor VIII (FVIII) deficiency due to hemophilia A.
Perioperative management in adult and pediatric patients with Type 1 and Type 2 von Willebrand Disease (VWD) in whom desmopressin (DDAVP) is either ineffective or contraindicated.
Perioperative management in minor surgery in adult and pediatric patients with Type 3 VWD.
