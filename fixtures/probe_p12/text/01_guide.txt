For doctors: how far to trust each part
Source. Open Targets Platform.
“Approved”. In at least one country, per the database. Not a measure of efficacy.
Left out. Withdrawn anywhere, or approved nowhere: counted, never named. Unclear link to the disease: counted separately.
Wider and narrower diseases. Shown with a label, each in its own folded box.
Known gaps. A missing medicine may be withdrawn somewhere, filed further away, or not recorded yet.
Weak proof. Japan-list-only approvals say so. Approvals with no regulator record say so. Confirmed mistakes carry a red warning.
Ranking. Link strength across the top 25 proteins. One protein machine counts once.
AI. Gemini suggests which disease you mean, and Open Targets confirms it.
