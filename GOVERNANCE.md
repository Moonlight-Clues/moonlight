Moonlight is not a company and is not incorporated. It is not medical advice.
Open Targets does not endorse or partner with this project.

## Roles

- **Product lead:** Kashish Dugar. Sets product direction and the standard
  for what appears on a card.
- **Co-founder and maintainer:** Shreshth Dugar. Operates the GitHub
  organisation with the other organisation admins, reviews and merges
  changes, and is a proposed Open Collective administrator.
- **Design:** Sidha Mogha. Leads design, and is a proposed Open Collective
  administrator.

Open Collective administrators, if Open Source Collective accepts the
project, will be Shreshth Dugar and Sidha Mogha. They will have authority
to manage the collective settings, approve expenses, and manage the budget,
as described by Open Source Collective. Kashish Dugar is credited as
product lead and is not an Open Collective administrator.

## Decisions

- Product and editorial decisions (what the site says, and what is shown
  on a card) are led by the product lead, with the maintainer.
- Changes to the public repository are merged by a maintainer, after
  review.
- If Open Source Collective hosts the project, spending of collective
  funds must be approved by an Open Collective administrator, and must
  follow OSC's expense rules.
- If a maintainer or Open Collective administrator steps down, the
  remaining people in the roles above appoint a replacement. If no
  agreement can be reached, the product lead and the maintainer decide
  together.
